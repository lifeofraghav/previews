About

A skyline is easy. A community that endures for generations is the real architecture.

Established in 1978, Hiranandani Group is one of the most prestigious real estate developers based in Mumbai, with the vision of creating a new India with self-sufficient and enduring townships.

Every activity, be it in real estate, education, healthcare, hospitality, leisure or entertainment, has steadily focused on creating better communities, from Hiranandani Gardens in Powai to Hiranandani Estate and One Hiranandani Park in Thane.
