About

Great skylines are not bought. They are earned, one uncompromising tower at a time.

Tiger Group was founded in 1976 with the inspiring vision of its founders to effectively participate in the building of the United Arab Emirates by delivering landmark projects to the highest possible quality standards that exceed customers' expectations.

Tiger Group is your trusted real estate company in the UAE for a unique future of modernity, classiness and luxury. We create practical homes and winning investment plans, and provide world-class lifestyles.
