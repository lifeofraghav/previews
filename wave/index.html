About Wave Estate

A township is not measured in acres. It is measured in mornings you never want to leave.

Wave Estate is a distinguished address strategically located at the heart of Mohali, in Sectors 85 and 99. Spanning 235 acres, the gated community offers contemporary villas, plots, premium group housing, independent floors, SCOs, commercial areas, community centres and schools.

The IGBC pre-certified Silver-rated green township features internationally designed open green spaces by renowned consultant ATKINS UK, with round-the-clock surveillance and an elite community club offering a gym, pool, home theatre, library and more.
