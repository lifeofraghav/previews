About Goel Ganga

Pune is ranked India's happiest city. Somebody has to build the homes that keep it that way.

Goel Ganga Developments is a brand synonymous with quality and timely delivery. For over 40 years, the group has consistently delivered landmark projects across Pune, from north to south and west to east, offering a choice of location, design, size and price in India's happiest city.

After creating its niche in Pune realty, the group expanded into a multifaceted conglomerate spanning education, healthcare, energy and logistics, each vertical led by dynamic visionaries and grounded in transparent, customer-centric trade practices.
