About SBP

A promise kept fifteen thousand times is not luck. It is a system.

SBP Group is a trusted name in the real estate market of the Tricity region, named the No. 1 housing company in Punjab. Having delivered over 15,000 homes in 18 years, SBP leads the region through a deep understanding of buyer demand and market sentiment, via innovation and research.

From Mohali's 200 acres of parks and its international airport to Zirakpur on the Chandigarh-Delhi highway and fast-rising Derabassi, SBP builds where the Tricity grows, with a stated ambition of delivering one lakh dream home keys in the next 14 years.
