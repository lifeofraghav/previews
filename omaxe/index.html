About

India does not need more buildings. It needs places worth gathering in.

The brand Omaxe was founded in 1987 by visionary first-generation entrepreneur and civil engineer Mr. Rohtaas Goel to undertake construction and contracting business. The company diversified into real estate in 2001 and got listed on both stock exchanges, BSE and NSE, in 2007.

At Omaxe, we envision creating a better tomorrow for India: skylines across Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities with a deep commitment to quality and customer satisfaction, under the leadership of Managing Director Mr. Mohit Goel.
